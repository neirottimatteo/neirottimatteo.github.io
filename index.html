03 Machine learning · Research

Recession Probability Engine

A monthly probability of a US recession, built on 58 features drawn from ~60 FRED series going back to 1939. 0.959 average out-of-sample AUC, 13.2 months of advance warning, no sustained false alarms. Every prediction is decomposed with SHAP, and 25 candidate features were tested and rejected on the evidence.

About

Engineer first, founder second.

I started with electronics and automation at ITI Ettore Majorana, graduating with 100/100. By twenty I was inside the Stellantis Safety Center in Orbassano, where for nearly three years I owned crash-test data — an environment where a wrong number is not a bug, it is a safety and regulatory problem.

That left me with a useful obsession: data has to be verifiable. It is the same idea behind everything I have built since — a timing system that states its accuracy and proves it, a macro model that explains every single prediction instead of asking for trust, a product that reads financial filings and always points back to the source.

In September 2025 I left Stellantis to build Kleriox full time. Alongside it I study electronic engineering and keep pushing CRONO forward, because I still like soldering.
