Recession Probability Engine

A monthly probability of a US recession, built only on public data. The current model has an AUC of 0.957, caught all six recessions between 1971 and 2025 with about 11.8 months of warning, and has not given a false alarm since 1978. The paper is on GitHub.

About

The short version

I got my diploma in electronics and automation at ITI Ettore Majorana, with 100/100. At twenty I started at the Stellantis Safety Center in Orbassano, where I worked on crash-test data for almost three years. In September 2025 I left to enrol at Politecnico di Torino, and studying is now my main occupation.

Kleriox started in September 2025. I am co-founder and CEO, and I work on it next to my studies: the product, the architecture and the strategy.

I also built Libella, a small AI assistant to help my father with a few things at his architecture studio. It was a side project and I am not working on it any more.

Economics

Economics started as curiosity and now shapes most of what I build. I follow the US economy closely, and the recession model and the Kleriox app both come from that.

I like the parts that can be measured: what really leads a cycle, which indicators still hold out of sample, and how much of a quarterly report is signal and how much is noise.

I also invest, and I enjoy it a lot. I research companies in a value-investing style.

AI

I use AI to read technical documents and to draft code and text. I see it as something that lets you do much more. I think it will be a frontier technology for the coming years, and being able to use it will matter as much as being able to use a computer, maybe more.

I am also interested in the technical side. I read about how it works and follow new advances, to stay up to date.
