Studio Neirotti website

Built alone For my father's studio architettoneirotti.eu ↗

The website of my father's architecture studio. I built it alone in 2023, before I used AI for coding, and I still look after it.

What it is

It is a site in Italian with the pages a studio needs: who they are, what they do, the completed projects with photographs, a gallery and a contact page. The projects and photos live in a database managed from Django's admin area, so adding a project does not mean touching the code.

How it is built

Backend
Django (Python) with SQLite and Pillow for the images
Frontend
Django templates with HTML and CSS, and GSAP for the animations on some pages
Contact
Form with reCAPTCHA and a cookie banner
Hosting
PythonAnywhere

Before AI, and after

The first commit is from April 2023 and the site went online in June. I did everything myself, with no help from AI: structure, database, pages, styling. In 2025 I made a few small updates, like the new logo and visit statistics.

In 2026 AI helped me improve it, mainly the graphics: the logo that draws itself, the photos with slanted edges, the project lists. With it I also fixed some smaller things, like the contact form, which had stopped sending emails. The site is still the 2023 one, and the foundation is the same.
